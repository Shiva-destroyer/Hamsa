# Demo sample packs

Generated by `python api/scripts/make_demo_packs.py --verify` (needs the seeded DB) and laid out by `python api/scripts/print_sheet.py` → **`PRINT_ME.pdf`** (A4, 2 packs per page, 5 pages, 300 dpi). `QR_SHEET_15.pdf` (from `make_qr_sheet.py`) puts 15 mixed packs on a single A4 page; its cells are not committed and can be re-created with `make_qr_sheet.py --export`.

**Print at 100% on plain white matte paper and photograph the paper — never a screen** (moiré breaks QR decoding). All packs say "SAMPLE PACK – HAMSA DEMO – NOT A REAL MEDICINE".

If a phone-camera photo of the printed paper does not give the verdict above, re-shoot in daylight, whole pack in frame, or type the batch number instead. Phone-camera decode rates have not been measured.
